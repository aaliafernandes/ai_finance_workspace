# Costco (COST) — Buffett-style business-quality memo

**Verdict: own the business.** The five annual 10-K rows show revenue rising
from $226,954.0 million at 2022-08-28 to $303,154.0 million at 2026-08-30.
Net income rose from $5,844.0 million to $9,226.0 million, while owner earnings
rose from $3,501.0 million to $9,382.0 million.

The code reports ROE of 28.3%, 25.1%, 31.2%, 27.8%, and 25.8%; therefore the
15% ROE check passes in every reported year. Owner earnings are positive in
each row and grew from the first fiscal year to the last, so both owner-earnings
checks pass. This is consistent with a business that has displayed durable
profitability and cash generation in the available record.

The five-year 10-K evidence cannot by itself prove the durability of Costco's
economic moat or management quality. Those remain judgment questions and should
be revisited with business evidence beyond these computed facts. This memo does
not judge the share price.

**Trust decision:** I trust this business-quality verdict provisionally because
every computed check passes and the printed revenue, net income, and owner
earnings trend upward; I would not treat it as a price-based investment decision.
