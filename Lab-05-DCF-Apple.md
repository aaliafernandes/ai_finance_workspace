## Interpretation and limitations

The five-year growth sequence and terminal growth are forecasts, not historical
facts. The reverse-DCF shift is one set of assumptions consistent with a target
price while holding the other model inputs fixed; it is not proof that Apple is
mispriced. The largest present limitation is the missing cash-interest-paid input,
which prevents a fully sourced FCFF calculation under the lab's required formula.

### Reasonableness (V)

The model value of **$(4.08)** per diluted share is outside the required
0.5x–2x range around the September 10, 2026 AAPL price of **$315.34**. No input
was adjusted to force agreement. The single input distrusted most is **starting
FCFF**, because the unresolved 100.0 training placeholder is in USD millions,
whereas Apple reported operating cash flow of $111,482 million before capex and
the required after-tax interest-paid add-back. This is a scale mismatch, not a
valuation conclusion.
