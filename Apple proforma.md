## Decision and intended user

This Edition A is an initial research memorandum for an investment committee deciding whether to initiate research coverage of Apple Inc. and, after further valuation work, whether an investment is warranted.

**Initial decision: Watch — defer initiation.** Apple’s FY2025 filing evidences durable scale, substantial profitability, and a growing, high-margin Services business. However, this Edition A does not yet establish an acceptable valuation-adjusted expected return. The recommendation is therefore to continue diligence rather than initiate a position on the evidence below alone.

### Operating-value driver

Services is the selected operating-value driver. In FY2025, Services net sales grew 14% to $109.158 billion and Services gross margin was 75.4%, compared with total company sales growth of 6%. The filing attributes Services growth principally to advertising, the App Store, and cloud services. This makes the durability of Services growth and Services margin a consequential input to any valuation.

## Initial thesis

Apple combines a large installed ecosystem with hardware-led revenue and an expanding Services stream. The FY2025 filing supports a preliminary view that Services can matter disproportionately to value because its revenue growth outpaced company-wide sales growth and its reported gross-margin percentage exceeded the Products gross-margin percentage. Apple also generated $112.010 billion of net income in FY2025 while maintaining material liquidity.

The counterweight is that the filing identifies meaningful risks to demand, margin, and ecosystem economics. These include intense competition and pricing pressure; tariff and trade restrictions; exposure to component supply and pricing; reliance on third-party developers and distribution partners; and regulatory, tax, and data-security risks. The filing also reports a year-over-year decline in Greater China net sales. Without a valuation model, a return hurdle, and deeper evidence on the persistence of Services growth, the appropriate initial posture is Watch — defer.

## Consequential assumptions

1. Services growth can remain a meaningful positive contributor to revenue mix and gross-margin resilience; this requires continued customer engagement and acceptable economics in advertising, the App Store, cloud services, and other offerings.
2. Product demand, particularly iPhone demand, can remain sufficiently resilient to support the broader ecosystem and Services attach opportunity.
3. Competitive pricing, tariffs, foreign exchange, component costs, and product mix do not reduce consolidated margins enough to offset operating growth.
4. Apple can continue to deploy capital through repurchases and dividends without impairing operating flexibility or increasing financial risk beyond the committee’s tolerance.
5. The class bridge definition of debt and cash is appropriate for the intended valuation framework.

## Unknowns and open diligence items

- The class-supplied synthetic enterprise value, known equity-value answer, and any required bridge adjustments.
- The market price, diluted share convention, valuation multiple/DCF assumptions, and required return as of the selected valuation date.
- The durability of Services growth, including regulatory exposure affecting App Store and advertising economics.
- The extent and duration of tariff, trade, supply-chain, and foreign-exchange effects on product margins.
- The outlook for Greater China demand and competitive intensity.
- The contribution, costs, monetization path, and competitive effect of AI-related product and service features.

## Research plan

1. Complete and reconcile the class synthetic enterprise-to-equity bridge using the class-provided inputs and document every adjustment.
2. Build a three-case operating forecast for Products and Services, with explicit revenue growth, gross-margin, operating-expense, tax, capital-expenditure, and working-capital assumptions.
3. Test valuation under both discounted cash flow and comparable-multiple approaches; show the implied value per diluted share and sensitivity to key assumptions.
4. Analyze Services growth and margin drivers separately, including regulatory and competitive downside cases.
5. Assess China exposure, supply-chain concentration, tariff sensitivity, capital-allocation policy, and the sustainability of share repurchases.
6. Identify one or more falsification conditions that would change the initial Watch — defer recommendation to Do Not Initiate.
